## 1. Verdict on the research question as posed

**Not well-posed yet.** Each load-bearing phrase lacks a measurable referent, the existing data was collected for its dual (source varied, target fixed), and the question is degenerate at both ends of k on the data that exists.

| phrase | why it fails | what must be fixed |
|---|---|---|
| "apply well" | No metric, no admissible set, no deployment budget. Every headline transplant relaxes durability (15/15 dw OFF). The fully-durable region has only been searched one knob at a time: best **target-side** fully-durable measurement is **854 tps (4%)** (`history_eval_replay_S0_sweep.json` row 1, redo 806 MB, sb=1/flush=1/dw=ON); source-side best 3,392 is a single draw; the 12 fully-durable rows in the non-sweep 800k pools max at 282; `sync_binlog=0` was never sampled anywhere. "One shot" in the evidence is best-of-3 with 3 restarts (~20 min; top-1 alone mean 69%, min 28%). | q_B = best-of-B on-target TPS ÷ a *validated* reference optimum on θ*, under a declared admissibility policy X_adm (durability-relaxed vs pinned), with B stated; **B=1 remains the headline tier** (HANDOFF §1 thesis), best-of-3 is the fallback tier. |
| "fairly confident" | Three randomness sources (target draw from belief π, measurement noise, collection seed) are conflated; the unit of replication for the RQ is the *target*, of which there is exactly one; noise is config-class dependent (top config 18,905–19,772 ≈4%; a mid-range config measured 4× in eval2 s44 spans 1,568–3,547 = 2.3×; the "lossless" 20,180 re-measures 18,306/17,706/18,766, mean of four 18,740 ≈ 94%). | Nested statement P_{θ*∼π}[ P_ε[q_B ≥ τ] ≥ 1−δ_noise ] ≥ 1−δ (τ=0.8, δ=0.1, δ_noise=0.2), used consistently (the draft's boxed reformulation dropped the noise layer — fixed below), plus a robust tier (min over supp π); one confidence level throughout: one-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson gives LB 0.74 for 10/10 and 0.90 for 29/29 (the draft's 0.69 was the 97.5% one-sided value). |
| "minimal number" | Count is not the decision variable. (a) The only placement effect that clears noise is *avoid the scale-mismatched S3 space-filling cells*; the 97/99/101 "mismatched beats S0" ordering is inside the ±20-point noise band (the same config measured 9,605 and 19,514 in two sessions). (b) Cost per testing workload varies 6.5–28.4 node-h per 100 successes (`logs/parallel/collect_S*.log`), driven by collection protocol, failure rate and data scale. (c) Risk is non-monotone in k under a naive rule (raw-source-TPS top-3, B=3: mean q 0.817 at k=1 → 0.736 at k=7 → 0.883 at k=15; min 0.476 for k=2..14), and **even oracle placement is non-monotone**: adding the identical-workload S0-llama cell to S4-llama lowers q from 97% to 80% because S0-llama's raw and normalized scores outrank S4's and its transplants were 80%. The k-curve is really risk-vs-(ranking rule, pool quality). | Min-cost covering problem; k is an *output*; the ranking/selection rule ρ and identification error are part of the formal object. |
| degenerate ends | k=1: at the workload level, taking each workload's best strategy gives S0 97 / S1 101 / S2 96 / S3 88 / S4 99 — every workload keeps ≥88% at k=1, but only with the strategy chosen post hoc; at the cell level P(q≥0.8 \| correctly-scaled 800k cell, k=1) = **9/12 = 0.75** (S2-lhs 62, S1-llama 61, S2-random 79, S0-llama 80), below the 1−δ = 0.9 target. The ≥88% floor holds only for the three **round-2** llama cells (S2/S3/S4: 96/88/97); the round-1 llama cells kept 80% (S0) and 61% (S1) and are protocol-confounded (I6). "Two ≥96%" holds for only 3 of 10 llama pairs under raw top-3 ranking. k=0: the rule-based baseline (`innodb_dedicated_server`-style / relaxed-durability preset) was never run, so necessity of *any* testing workload is unproven; offline, the 76 recipe-satisfying 800k configs in `pool_ALL` span p10 6.2k / median 10.6k / p75 13.8k / max 19.9k, so the rule's outcome is not predictable from current data. | Run k=0 first; redesign the ladder so the optimum genuinely moves; draw the k-curve over *workloads with strategy fixed*, not over the 15 strategy×workload cells. |

**Proposed reformulation (use this wording):**

> Given a template-closed OLTP workload whose transactions are fixed and whose parameters θ = (rows/table, client concurrency, access skew, read fraction) are uncertain under an operator belief π; a config-admissibility policy X_adm (in particular the durability setting); a deployment budget of B on-target evaluations (B=1 headline, B=3 fallback); a selection rule ρ that maps a short window of live counters plus the library to B candidates; and a per-workload collection cost c(θ, C) under collection protocol C (θ-dependence through data scale) — find the cheapest set T of imitation workloads, each contributing a stored top-K portfolio, such that P_{θ*∼π}[ P_ε[ q_B(T, ρ; θ*) ≥ τ ] ≥ 1−δ_noise ] ≥ 1−δ, where q_B is the best-of-B on-target throughput relative to a validated reference optimum on θ* (measured by an own collection on θ* for the guarantee subset; where only library-relative q is available, the statement is a *selection* guarantee and must be labelled as such). Characterize the per-dimension transfer regions R_τ(θ_i) — regime boundaries (working set vs buffer pool, IO- vs CPU-bound) rather than metric radii — that make |T| computable from π, and show that the identification of θ* needed by ρ is achievable from directly observable counters, including from a running non-default configuration.

Sub-questions: Q1 per-dimension radius/regime (both directions, ≥3 magnitudes); Q2 composition of simultaneous mismatches; Q3 q vs |T| under fixed B and a stated ρ; Q4 live identification accuracy (parametric vs metric-vector, cold vs warm pool); Q5 the k=0 and durability-pinned controls. The paper's central figure is the risk-vs-k curve under several ρ, never a single k. Note explicitly that X_adm, ρ and c(θ,C) are additions to your framing motivated by I1, I5 and the failure-rate data.

## 3. The most important issues and decisions

Lens disagreements resolved: (a) *formalization* ("k is the wrong objective") vs *experimental design* ("k is the output of a pre-registered rule") — both: min-cost cover, k reported as output, k-curve as figure, with ρ inside the formal object. (b) F3 "genuine re-ranking" vs *measurement* ("noise") — measurement wins for space-filling cells; genuine re-ranking exists only across the scale boundary (`6bdc9258` 1→16). (c) "selection barely matters" vs "selector error makes risk non-monotone" — fine similarity is not load-bearing; the scale gate, pool quality and the ranking rule are. (d) M=10 vs M≥29 targets — sequence them. (e) "anisotropy is an InnoDB artefact" vs "scale is the dominant breaker" — present scale as a regime boundary through doublewrite and bp-vs-working-set; stronger and repairable.

### I1. Durability trade + missing k=0 baseline — fatal if unaddressed
**Problem.** Every high-TPS transplant relaxes crash safety. Pooled 800k data (sync_binlog>1, qabs<32 MiB): dw OFF/flush 0-2 median 8,076 (n=188) vs dw ON ≈2,028 (n=170); best flush=1 & dw=ON source config 7,612 (38%); best fully-durable target-side measurement 854 (4%), from single-knob sweeps only; no joint fully-durable search exists; `sync_binlog=0` never sampled. `innodb_dedicated_server` and `innodb_flush_method` are absent from both `mysql_perf_8.0.json` (117) and `mysql_all_8044_validated.json` (186). No rule-based one-shot was ever run, and the offline recipe spread (6.2k–19.9k) means its outcome is unpredictable. A launch/surge is exactly when nobody flips durability off on a primary.
**Recommendation.** (1) Three rule-based configs × 3 repeats: durable (default + max redo + bp 30 GB); relaxed (same + dw OFF/flush 2/sync_binlog 0); and a `dedicated_server`-style formula. **Feasibility fix:** replaying `innodb_dedicated_server=ON` inside the 117-knob space is a no-op (ConfigSpace requires full configurations, `_gen_config_file` writes every knob, so bp/log-file are always explicit and MySQL ignores dedicated_server for explicitly set variables) — compute the formula by hand (bp ≈ 0.75×RAM, redo from bp, O_DIRECT_NO_FSYNC via `sysbench_extra_args`/cnf) and replay explicit values. (2) Re-run the 15 winners with flush=1, sync_binlog=1, dw=ON (×2). (3) One durability-pinned LlamaTune collection on S0 (delete the three knobs from a copy of the JSON; `my.cnf.clean` carries only paths so mysqld defaults 1/1/ON apply) and transplant its top-5. (4) Make X_adm an operator input and report two tracks. If pinned BO configs beat the durable rule, I1 becomes a strength; if the relaxed rule reaches ~15–20k, Phases 1–2 have no measured value on this target.

### I2. The ladder cannot answer the RQ: one magnitude, one direction, no joint cells, coverage-confounded, degenerate k
**Problem.** S1–S4 are single steps, always source-smaller/less-skewed/read-heavier; no 2-D cell; round-1 vs round-2 protocol and stop rule confounded with S-level; kept% is coverage-confounded for round-2 space-filling cells; at the workload level every workload keeps ≥88% under its best strategy; no shared probe set links the target to S2/S3/S4.
**Recommendation.** Radius grid around physical thresholds: rows {80k, 200k (fits default pool), 400k (fits tuned pools only), 800k, 1.6M (≈RAM; pilot first — MIYABI.md §5 warns 60-s warmup understates large pools)}, clients {32, 64, 128, 256}, zipf {uniform, 0.3, 0.7, 1.0}, ps {2, 10, 30, 60}; all LlamaTune, round-2 protocol; recollect S0/S1-llama under round 2. Add D1 (80k+uniform) and D2 (800k + 32 clients + uniform + ps30). **Run one shared 30-config LHS probe set on every cell including S0** so rank agreement and config-matched metrics are measured target-vs-cell, not by elimination. Decompose the scale effect per knob and run the S3 repair test (rescale bp/dw by data-size ratio, ~10 evals). Frame Phase 1 as "one testing workload per regime cell the belief straddles" and report cross-cell effects with coverage-independent metrics (shared-config rank, target/source ratio, "known target configs that beat it": S1-random 2, S4-lhs 2, S0-lhs 9, S3-lhs 47 over 208 target SUCCESS rows).

### I3. Phase 3 selector: metric-vector similarity is the wrong tool under template closure
**Problem.** Template closure is not exploited: OT/DML/RF score rates and environment while three of four parameters are directly readable; OT 1/32 offline, DML hardware-blind (NN shares hardware 20% vs 57%), RF picks TPC-C for every sysbench target. Every selector ran on a cold pool at the default after a restart; identification under a non-default running config — the surge condition — was never tested, and the scale/skew signals (pages, hit%) are cold-cache artefacts by construction. "One shot" is best-of-3. The robust-config cross-validation signal is an artefact (above).
**Recommendation.** First run the cheap control: OT binning with nuisance dims dropped (O3c) — it decides whether Phase 3 needs a new selector or metric hygiene. Then parametric identification with the Phase-1 regime cells as the metric: `Threads_connected` **and** `Threads_running` (avg and max, reported separately), handler/row counters rather than the `topk(5)` Com_* slots (`prometheus_metrics.py:294`), `get_db_size()` wired as a hard scale gate, skew treated as cold-pool-identifiable only until the warm-pool test (needs a small code path: a metrics-only second iteration without `apply_knobs_online`, which would SET GLOBAL non-dynamic knobs and fail). Test the robustness signal on non-S3 configs / non-top-3 configs before making the ~40-eval cross-validation a Phase-2 requirement. Protocol: one restart + one 3-min acceptance window + fallback to #2; report top-1 and best-of-3 side by side; never write "one shot" without B.

### I4. Noise, circular denominator, n=1 everywhere
**Problem.** 19,940 (`scripts/report_eval.py:18`) is S0-random pool row 334, the max of 133 single draws, and re-measures 9,605/19,514; target-side singles reach 21,640 (`history_eval2_opadviser_ns_s43.json` it8). Kept% is max-of-3 single draws ÷ one single draw. Noise is config-class dependent: high-TPS configs median dev 3.6–4.0% with a 13–18% tail of >25% drops (9–11 configs, 28–45 measurements, definition above); mid-range configs up to 2.3×; default CV 16% (120.7–244.7, n=32). No transplant repeated by design (the shared-schedule pairs are the only repeats); one collection seed; one HeSBO projection. Hostnames are **recoverable** (first line `node=mcNNN` of every `logs/parallel/*.log`; one node per run) but confounded with cell; `Observation.info` is None.
**Recommendation.** Freeze a validated reference optimum: re-measure the top-10 target-side configs 3× (~3 node-h), reference = best median with CI. Report kept% against it *and* rank-based *and* absolute TPS with CIs, stratified by TPS level (O4). 3 repeats of every headline transplant, median (expected one-shot) and max (attainable); interleave replica lists across nodes (one select=3 job, three permuted orders — the current one-cell-per-node runners do not do this); add `socket.gethostname()` to `Observation.info` (one line in `pipleline.py`) and join existing histories to the log `node=` lines. Split-sample selection on the source (re-measure top-10 2×, select by median). Report kept% ÷ S0's own kept% so selection and measurement shrinkage cancel.

### I5. "Unbounded collection" is a motivation that was argued, not measured
**Problem.** No experiment varied repository size; the offline k-curve is unscripted; its non-monotonicity comes from S3's cache-resident 25–32k source maxima, its "monotone under normalized ranking" from S3's 3.3× higher default, and even oracle placement is non-monotone (S0-llama+S4-llama 97→80). The binding constraint is the ranking rule and pool quality, not the count.
**Recommendation.** Replace the motivation with the measured claim: "existing tuners give no sufficiency rule; adding contexts under a raw-score rule lowers one-shot quality; a scale-gated rule (not default-normalization) removes the harmful cells; on this ladder one correctly-scaled context keeps ≥80% with probability 0.75 — insufficient for δ=0.1." Commit the k-curve script (O0/O1), draw it over workloads with strategy fixed, add a second curve where the harmful cell is not distinguishable by default TPS, then add the samples-per-context axis via the truncated-prefix replay.

### I6. Collection-protocol confound and single-budget-point in Phase 2
**Problem.** F6 compares round-2 llama (96/88/97) against round-1-handicapped S0-llama (80 samples, FAILED labels in the inner BO) and unusable S1-llama (38); rounds also differ in stop rule. Winners at SUCCESS #14–99 (median 72); S4-llama still rising at the stop; llama's edge is partly "learned to avoid one knob" (trap 0.17–0.48 vs 0.66–0.75).
**Recommendation.** Recollect S0/S1-llama under round 2; truncated-prefix replay (N=25/50/75, ~135 evals) — O5 first checks whether prefix top-3 are already among measured replays; two extra seeds for S0/S3-llama (new ini `rand_seed` = new projections) or a pool-bootstrap of top-k stability; report the qabs trap explicitly as an interaction single-knob sweeps cannot see (the honest content of F2). Cheap cost lever: the 600-s connect wait makes each FAILED attempt ~9–10 min vs ~5 min for a SUCCESS (S4-llama's 125 failures ≈ 19 of its 28.4 h); with chunked datadirs already removing streaks, a `mysqld --validate-config` pre-check or a 180–240 s wait saves 20–35% of llama-cell cost across M0/M2/M3/M4/M7 (~60–100 node-h).

### I7. Safety, objective, and production hazards are design intent, not evidence
**Problem.** No constraint/rollback code; unconstrained probing in the shootout: 67% of iterations <80% of incumbent, 10 outages; 6–7 GB-redo configs change crash-recovery time (a production risk); TPS-only, `lat` (sysbench p95, `parser.py:89`) recorded but unused.
**Recommendation.** Either implement the minimum viable safe probe (candidate set = portfolio ∪ near-ties ∪ single-knob moves on `dynamic: Yes` knobs only; acceptance = one 3-min window vs the applied config with fixed margin; rollback of dynamic values; never restart into an untested config in production) and measure degraded-iteration count against an OnlineTune-style constrained-BO arm — or drop challenge (c) from the claims. OpAdviser's jittered init (19.1–19.5k, CV ≈1.8%) is already evidence that small moves around a validated incumbent are safe. Add p95 latency at zero cost: winners 9.9–31.4 ms (median ≈15 ms) vs 1,648–3,912 ms at default.

### I8. Positioning hazards: replay is preempted, template closure does no work, magnitudes are Lustre-specific
**Problem.** Every win came from OpAdviser's released `get_max_distence_best` replay (`pipleline.py:354-367`, commit `8ca5a99`); meta-learned warm-start from prior incumbents is Feurer et al. Nothing varies the template (17/17 inis `oltp_read_write`); the knob-level reasons transplants work are template-independent; in the 12-knob study a 100-wh TPC-C source was the best pick for sysbench rw50 (Jaccard 0.889, `top_percent.md:57`; same-type sources 0.09–0.21 at top-5%). **Recommendation.** Do not claim replay; cite OpAdviser's code as the baseline you explain (F7a as community service). ## 4. Novelty positioning

Transfer-based tuners (OtterTune SIGMOD'17, ResTune SIGMOD'21, OpAdviser VLDB'24) presuppose an accumulated repository and search it for a similar task; none asks which workloads should be *run* to populate it, none gives a sufficiency rule, and — as measured here — the surrogate route through which the repository is supposed to act converts nothing under a wall-clock budget even with the identical workload present (OT mapping and RGPE ≈ cold at 1 h), while the mechanism that works, replaying source-best configurations, is undocumented in the one system that uses it. We invert the problem: under template closure the operator's belief over workload parameters defines the space of imitation workloads, and we ask for the cheapest set whose stored top-K portfolios transfer in ≤B evaluations. This is the algorithm-configuration portfolio problem (Hydra AAAI'10, ISAC ECAI'10, Auto-sklearn 2.0's greedy portfolio, zero-shot HPO) transported to a regime where each meta-train task costs 10–28 node-hours, evaluations can poison state, and transfer is anisotropic with regime boundaries; we measure per-axis transfer on MySQL 8.0/117 knobs, show that scale mismatch fails through two identifiable knobs, that live identification reduces to a handful of directly observable counters where metric-vector binning fails the oracle test, and use these to bound |T| — with the sim2real stopping rule (SPOTA) and the scenario approach (Calafiore–Campi) supplying the form of the sufficiency rule. (Keep out until fixed: "one-shot", "deterministic", "minimal number", "lossless", and any performance number without the durability-pinned track.)

**Three closest works and the differentiator**
1. **OpAdviser (PVLDB 17(3))** — exogenous, unbounded repository (3,510 tasks, 48 labelled, ~20 days on 4 instances, p.9–10), concordance ≥0.5 similarity that needs target observations first, and a replay init the paper does not describe. Differentiator: we *choose* which tasks to run from a belief and cost model, identify from a 3-min default-config window, and show the measured 1-h gain is the replay of one context (space-only median 6.8k vs 19.3k), quantifying winner's curse (rank-1 best in 6/15) as the reason for top-K.
2. **E2ETune (PVLDB 18(13), 2025)** — the only knob-tuning paper that synthesizes workloads deliberately, but learns a generic workload→config mapping with a cost-model proxy. Differentiator: real evaluations on target-shaped workloads on target hardware, where the dominant effects (doublewrite on Lustre, the qabs trap) are invisible to a cost model; a coverage rule rather than a mapping. Must be run (with GPTuner/`innodb_dedicated_server`) as a k=0 baseline.
3. **OtterTune + the VLDB'21 Inquiry** — workload mapping from binned metrics is Phase 3's ancestor; tuning a replayed copy then applying to production is the practice precedent. Differentiator: OT binning never ranks the identical workload first with 15 same-hardware contexts and EI washes selection out (byte-identical proposals across five sources); the Inquiry replays a *captured* trace, we address pre-launch/surge with no trace. Second tier: ISAC (cluster→configure→assign is structurally the pipeline; we design rather than sample instances and measure the radius), Auto-sklearn 2.0 (adopt its greedy portfolio; novelty is task design under cost), CDBTune/Hunter/OnlineTune (need production traffic; our safety layer is unimplemented, present as design + the constraint gap).

## 6. Three biggest risks to the thesis and what would falsify it

1. **The gains are a crash-safety policy trade, and a rule needs no testing workload.** Falsified if: the relaxed-durability rule config (default + dw OFF + flush 2 + sync_binlog 0 + max redo + bp 30 GB — note sync_binlog=0 has never been measured) reaches ≥0.8 of the validated optimum on most sampled targets (then Phases 1–2 add nothing measurable; the offline recipe spread 6.2k–19.9k makes this outcome genuinely open), *or* durability-pinned transplants fall below τ and a pinned BO collection cannot recover them (then the measured transferability was mostly policy). Either outcome forces a reframe to durability-safe gains only.
2. **k is trivially 1 (or the design rule has no content).** Falsified if: A1(k=1) already ≥0.9 across the belief sample and A1 ≈ random placement at every k (rule adds nothing over "load production-scale data" — currently P=0.75 at k=1 among correctly-scaled cells, so this is not yet the case); or the opposite — "collect everything" beats A1 by >10 pts for all k≤4 at realistic belief spread; or a "free" dimension breaks within 2× (P(q≥0.8)<0.8 at 64 clients, zipf 0.3/1.0, ps2/ps60), or D2 keeps <80% while its 1-D components keep ≥91% (radii do not compose; k grows multiplicatively). The survivable version is the regime-cell rule, which requires the scale boundary to be real on both sides (200k/400k/1.6M cells) and the k-curve to be re-drawn with strategy fixed and coverage controlled.
3. **Phase 3 cannot identify or apply in one shot under production conditions, and template closure is doing no work.** Falsified if: no live selector — including the parametric one and OT-with-hygiene (O3c) — recognizes a same-workload sibling ≥80% of the time from a *running non-default* config on a *warm* pool, or mis-picks include scale-mismatched cells (then k restarts are needed and the cost model changes); or top-1 stays ~69%/min 28% after selection improvements (then "one shot" must be sold as B-shot with ~20 min); or sysbench-collected configs transplant to the TPC-C target at ≥88% (template closure is neither necessary nor sufficient; Phase 1 must be reframed around data scale and durability policy rather than transaction templates).
